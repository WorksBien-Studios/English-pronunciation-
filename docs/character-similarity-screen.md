# Character similarity screen — 音の島

**Date:** 2026-09-29  
**Scope:** the guide character (コーチ) and the six sound creatures in [`game-ui-concept.md`](game-ui-concept.md)  
**Method:** visual comparison against well-known Japanese characters plus targeted web searches (Dragon Quest Slime, Kirby, existing English-learning creature apps, chat-bubble mascots).  
**This is a screening step, not legal clearance.** It did not include a trademark-database search (J-PlatPat / JPO, USPTO, EUIPO), a design-registration search or an app-store character search, and no lawyer reviewed it.

## Findings and actions

| Item | Risk found | Action taken |
|---|---|---|
| ぺろ (/l/): blue round body with a small pointed peak | Evokes the Dragon Quest **Slime** (Square Enix): blue, peaked top, smiling face. | Body changed to a lighter sky-cyan; the peak replaced by a straight antenna with a pink bulb (a tongue-tip hint). |
| ぷっぷ (/b/): pink round body, pink blush cheeks, dark oval eyes | Evokes **Kirby** (Nintendo): pink sphere, blush cheeks, dark oval eyes. | Body changed to warm cream; pink kept only for cheeks; bubble feature retained. |
| くるりん | Same as the title *Kuru Kuru Kururin* (Nintendo). | Renamed **くるん**. |
| がぶ | Same as **ガブ**, the wolf in *あらしのよるに*. | Renamed **はむ**. |
| ふーふー (/f/): round body with a leaf on top | Leaf/sprout motifs are common (e.g. Pikmin's leaf); ours is a different silhouette. | Low risk, kept. Re-check in the trademark search. |
| べー (/θ/): round ears + winking tongue-out | てへぺろ is a generic expression (also associated with Fujiya's Peko-chan). Ours has no matching features. | Low risk, kept. |
| はむ (/v/): amber body with pointed cat-like ears | Generic cat-eared blob. | Low risk, kept. |
| コーチ: speech bubble with a face and a wavy strand | No specific mascot found in searches; chat-bubble faces and antennae are generic. It also mirrors the app icon. | Kept. |
| Concept ("collect creatures on an island") | The Japanese app *English Monsters / イーモン* already uses monster characters and an "island" world for English learning. | Concept is not protectable on its own; we use "なかま / 図鑑", not "monster", and none of its characters. Avoid the word モンスター and the name イーモン in all copy and metadata. |

## Sources consulted

- [Slime (Dragon Quest)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slime_(Dragon_Quest)) — teardrop design by Akira Toriyama; series mascot of Square Enix's franchise.
- [Kirby (character)](https://simple.wikipedia.com/wiki/Kirby_(character)) — small pink sphere with cheek blushes and oval eyes.
- [English Monsters (イーモン)](https://englishhub.jp/app/english-monsters.html) — existing English-learning app with monsters on "Eimon Island".

## Still required before the art ships

1. A trademark and design-registration search (J-PlatPat / JPO first; USPTO and EUIPO if distributing outside Japan) for the final character names: くるん, ぺろ, べー, はむ, ふーふー, ぷっぷ, and for the guide (currently unnamed).
2. An illustrator's original final artwork; the current SVG art is a mockup and should not be treated as final.
3. A repeat similarity review of the final art, since the screen above compares the mockup only.
4. Keep the exclusions in Apple guideline 5.2 in mind: do not use any third-party character, name or likeness, including "inspired by" work that is recognisably the same.
